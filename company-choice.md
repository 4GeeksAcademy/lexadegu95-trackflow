## Trackflow is my Company Choice
I chose TrackFlow because I see significant potential to increase annual revenue by integrating AI automation into its existing software and infrastructure. TrackFlow partners with well-established, high-end shipping carriers, making efficient and reliable technology essential for maintaining strong business relationships. Implementing AI-driven solutions could streamline operations, improve communication, and ultimately enhance customer satisfaction by creating a more seamless experience for both TrackFlow and its customers.
## My AI Agent Idea 
I propose an AI Returns Processing Agent for TrackFlow that would automatically review customer return requests and determine whether they qualify under each client's return policy. It would need access to customer orders, delivery dates, return reasons, and client-specific policies. The agent would approve eligible returns, generate shipping labels, schedule carrier collections, and notify customers of their return status. Unusual cases, such as international returns, would be sent to a human employee for review. This would reduce manual work, speed up returns, and improve efficiency.
